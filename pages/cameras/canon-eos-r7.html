Depth of Field for the Canon EOS R7

Depth of field is the range of distances in a scene that appears acceptably sharp in front of and behind the focus point. This page computes it for the Canon EOS R7 using the exact dimensions of its 22.3×14.9 mm sensor — no generic presets.

How to use this page

Find your lens and aperture, read the near–far range, and either compose within it or click the button to open the interactive calculator and drag the focus distance live. Landscape shooters should look at the hyperfocal column: set the lens to that distance and everything from half of it to infinity is sharp. Frequently asked questions

What is the circle of confusion for the Canon EOS R7?

0.018 mm — the standard sensor diagonal ÷ 1500 convention, applied to the Canon EOS R7's exact sensor.

What is the hyperfocal distance for a 50 mm lens on the Canon EOS R7?

About 17.5 m at f/8 — focus there and everything from 8.76 m to infinity is acceptably sharp.

Does the Canon EOS R7 have a crop factor?

1.61× — a 50 mm lens frames like a 81 mm lens on full frame. See the crop factor guide.
