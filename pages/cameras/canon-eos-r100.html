Sensor profile: what the Canon EOS R100 sensor means for depth of field

This is a crop sensor: the same lens frames tighter, so depth of field is deeper at matched framing — good for reach and handholdable landscapes, less so for the thinnest possible background melt.

How to use this page

Frequently asked questions

What is the diffraction limit of the Canon EOS R100?

About f/5.5. At a 3.72 µm effective pixel pitch there comes a point where the airy disc from an ever-smaller aperture covers more than a pixel can resolve, so sharpness stops improving. That is the number to stop at, not f/16 or f/22.

What is the circle of confusion for the Canon EOS R100?

0.018 mm — the standard sensor diagonal ÷ 1500 convention, applied to the Canon EOS R100's exact sensor.

What is the hyperfocal distance for a 50 mm lens on the Canon EOS R100?

About 17.5 m at f/8 — focus there and everything from 8.76 m to infinity is acceptably sharp.

Does the Canon EOS R100 have a crop factor?

1.61× — a 50 mm lens frames like a 81 mm lens on full frame. See the crop factor guide.
