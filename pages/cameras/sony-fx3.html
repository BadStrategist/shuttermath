Sensor profile: what the Sony FX3 sensor means for depth of field

A 36×24 mm chip is the reference size every other format is measured against, so its depth of field is the shallowest per framing and its sensors tolerate the smallest apertures before diffraction shows.

How to use this page

Frequently asked questions

What is the diffraction limit of the Sony FX3?

About f/12.7. At a 8.49 µm effective pixel pitch there comes a point where the airy disc from an ever-smaller aperture covers more than a pixel can resolve, so sharpness stops improving. That is the number to stop at, not f/16 or f/22.

What is the circle of confusion for the Sony FX3?

0.029 mm — the standard sensor diagonal ÷ 1500 convention, applied to the Sony FX3's exact sensor.

What is the hyperfocal distance for a 50 mm lens on the Sony FX3?

About 10.9 m at f/8 — focus there and everything from 5.44 m to infinity is acceptably sharp.

Does the Sony FX3 have a crop factor?

1.00× — a 50 mm lens frames like a 50 mm lens on full frame. See the crop factor guide.
