Why camera-specific?

Depth of field depends on the sensor's physical size, not the brand. Two 50 mm lenses at f/2.8 focused at 5 m give different near/far ranges on a full-frame body versus a Micro 4/3 body — so a calculator that doesn't know your sensor is guessing. Every page here is preset to one real body and deep-links into the interactive calculator.
