Depth of Field for the Panasonic Lumix S5 II

Depth of field is the range of distances in a scene that appears acceptably sharp in front of and behind the focus point. This page computes it for the Panasonic Lumix S5 II using the exact dimensions of its 35.6×23.8 mm sensor — no generic presets.

How to use this page

Find your lens and aperture, read the near–far range, and either compose within it or click the button to open the interactive calculator and drag the focus distance live. Landscape shooters should look at the hyperfocal column: set the lens to that distance and everything from half of it to infinity is sharp. Frequently asked questions

What is the circle of confusion for the Panasonic Lumix S5 II?

0.029 mm — the standard sensor diagonal ÷ 1500 convention, applied to the Panasonic Lumix S5 II's exact sensor.

What is the hyperfocal distance for a 50 mm lens on the Panasonic Lumix S5 II?

About 11.0 m at f/8 — focus there and everything from 5.50 m to infinity is acceptably sharp.

Does the Panasonic Lumix S5 II have a crop factor?

1.01× — a 50 mm lens frames like a 51 mm lens on full frame. See the crop factor guide.
