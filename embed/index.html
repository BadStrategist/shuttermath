Free Embeddable Photography Calculator Widgets

Add working photography calculators to your blog, tutorial, or gear review — free, no sign-up, no watermark. Pick a widget, copy the embed code, done.

Widgets

  • ND Filter Calculator — exact long-exposure shutter times (ND2–ND100000).
  • Star Trails Calculator — NPF / 500 / 300 / 600 rules, uses your camera's real pixel size.
  • Depth of Field Calculator — near, far, and total DoF for any lens.
  • Astro Pixel Scale Calculator — arcsec/pixel sampling vs your seeing.

Embed any widget

Each widget is one self-contained HTML file. Paste this on WordPress, Wix, Blogger, Squarespace, or any site that allows iframes:

<iframe src="https://shuttermath.com/embed/WIDGET.html"
  width="100%" height="480" style="border:1px solid #ddd;border-radius:10px"
  title="ShutterMath calculator" loading="lazy"></iframe>

Replace WIDGET.html with nd-filter-widget.html, star-trails-widget.html, dof-widget.html, or pixel-scale-widget.html.

Why use them? Your readers get genuinely useful, accurate tools — no registration wall. They stay current automatically, and a small "Calculated with ShutterMath" credit keeps the source transparent.
