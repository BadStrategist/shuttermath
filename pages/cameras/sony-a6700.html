Sensor profile: what the Sony A6700 sensor means for depth of field

This is a crop sensor: the same lens frames tighter, so depth of field is deeper at matched framing — good for reach and handholdable landscapes, less so for the thinnest possible background melt.

How to use this page

Frequently asked questions

What is the diffraction limit of the Sony A6700?

About f/5.7. At a 3.80 µm effective pixel pitch there comes a point where the airy disc from an ever-smaller aperture covers more than a pixel can resolve, so sharpness stops improving. That is the number to stop at, not f/16 or f/22.

What is the circle of confusion for the Sony A6700?

0.019 mm — the standard sensor diagonal ÷ 1500 convention, applied to the Sony A6700's exact sensor.

What is the hyperfocal distance for a 50 mm lens on the Sony A6700?

About 16.7 m at f/8 — focus there and everything from 8.33 m to infinity is acceptably sharp.

Does the Sony A6700 have a crop factor?

1.53× — a 50 mm lens frames like a 77 mm lens on full frame. See the crop factor guide.
