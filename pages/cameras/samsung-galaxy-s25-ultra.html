Depth of Field for the Samsung Galaxy S25 Ultra

Depth of field is the range of distances in a scene that appears acceptably sharp in front of and behind the focus point. This page computes it for the Samsung Galaxy S25 Ultra using the exact dimensions of its 9.8×7.4 mm sensor — no generic presets.

How to use this page

Find your lens and aperture, read the near–far range, and either compose within it or click the button to open the interactive calculator and drag the focus distance live. Landscape shooters should look at the hyperfocal column: set the lens to that distance and everything from half of it to infinity is sharp. Frequently asked questions

What is the circle of confusion for the Samsung Galaxy S25 Ultra?

0.008 mm — the standard sensor diagonal ÷ 1500 convention, applied to the Samsung Galaxy S25 Ultra's exact sensor.

What is the hyperfocal distance for a 50 mm lens on the Samsung Galaxy S25 Ultra?

About 38.2 m at f/8 — focus there and everything from 19.1 m to infinity is acceptably sharp.

Does the Samsung Galaxy S25 Ultra have a crop factor?

3.52× — a 50 mm lens frames like a 176 mm lens on full frame. See the crop factor guide.
