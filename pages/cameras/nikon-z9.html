Sensor profile: what the Nikon Z9 sensor means for depth of field

A 36×24 mm chip is the reference size every other format is measured against, so its depth of field is the shallowest per framing and its sensors tolerate the smallest apertures before diffraction shows.

Frequently asked questions

What is the diffraction limit of the Nikon Z9?

About f/6.5. At a 4.35 µm effective pixel pitch there comes a point where the airy disc from an ever-smaller aperture covers more than a pixel can resolve, so sharpness stops improving. That is the number to stop at, not f/16 or f/22.

What is the circle of confusion for the Nikon Z9?

0.029 mm — the standard sensor diagonal ÷ 1500 convention, applied to the Nikon Z9's exact sensor.

What is the hyperfocal distance for a 50 mm lens on the Nikon Z9?

About 10.9 m at f/8 — focus there and everything from 5.46 m to infinity is acceptably sharp.

Does the Nikon Z9 have a crop factor?

1.00× — a 50 mm lens frames like a 50 mm lens on full frame. See the crop factor guide.
