How to use this page

Find your lens and aperture, read the near–far range, and compose inside it — or open the interactive calculator to drag the focus distance live. On the Panasonic Lumix GH7 the practical ceiling is about f/4.5: stopping down past that deepens the sharp zone but starts costing detail to diffraction, so lean on the hyperfocal column instead of the smallest aperture on the dial. Frequently asked questions

What is the diffraction limit of the Panasonic Lumix GH7?

About f/4.5. At a 3.00 µm effective pixel pitch there comes a point where the airy disc from an ever-smaller aperture covers more than a pixel can resolve, so sharpness stops improving. That is the number to stop at, not f/16 or f/22.

What is the circle of confusion for the Panasonic Lumix GH7?

0.014 mm — the standard sensor diagonal ÷ 1500 convention, applied to the Panasonic Lumix GH7's exact sensor.

What is the hyperfocal distance for a 50 mm lens on the Panasonic Lumix GH7?

About 21.7 m at f/8 — focus there and everything from 10.9 m to infinity is acceptably sharp.

Does the Panasonic Lumix GH7 have a crop factor?

2.00× — a 50 mm lens frames like a 100 mm lens on full frame. See the crop factor guide.
